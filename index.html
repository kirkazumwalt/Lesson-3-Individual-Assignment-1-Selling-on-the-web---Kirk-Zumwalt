For Sale: Hallmark Keepsake Classic American Cars Collection

The Hallmark Keepsake Classic American Cars Series are detailed Christmas ornaments modeled after iconic cars.

Hallmark introduced the Hallmark Keepsake Ornament Classic American Car Series in 1991. The first Classic American Car was the 1957 Corvette. The Classic American Car series consists of the most well-known classic American cars in production. A few of the American cars in this series are 1969 Dodge Super Bee, 1970 Ford Torino, 1970 Ford Mustang Fastback, 1966 Ford Mustang, 1957 Chevrolet Belair, Oldsmobile Hurst 442, and 1970 Plymouth Hemi Cuda.

The Classic American Cars Series are great for Muscle Car fanatics!

The Classic American Cars Series have presents inside or hauling a Christmas tree to represent Christmas. The features of these cars consist of being very detailed, made of die-cast metal, and the front wheels turn. Each cars have two different license plates, one indicating the model year of the car, and one for the year the ornament was issued.
